FAQ

Questions about our products

Short answers on roasting, allergens and pricing.

  1. 01

    When are the nuts roasted?

    They are roasted in small daily batches; the roasting date is printed on the pack (fictional demo information).

  2. 02

    Is allergen information provided?

    Yes; products containing peanuts, hazelnuts and gluten are marked at the counter and on the label (demo text).

  3. 03

    Are the images and data on the site real?

    No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.

  4. 04

    How are prices determined?

    Prices depend on the product and weight; contact us for current prices. This demo has no price list.
